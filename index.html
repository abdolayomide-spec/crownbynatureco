Rituals

How to Use

For Hair

Warm a small amount between your palms, apply to scalp and hair, and massage gently to the roots. Use 3-4 times per week.

Scalp Treatment

Apply a thin layer directly to the scalp. Massage for 2-3 minutes, then leave in or rinse after 30 minutes.

For Skin

Apply a few drops to clean, damp skin and massage until absorbed to moisturize and soften.

Hot Oil Treatment

Warm the oil slightly (never overheat) and apply generously from the root through the lengths.

Good to know

Care, Storage & Safety

Getting the most from it

Start with a small amount and add more if needed. Warming a few drops between your palms makes it easier to spread. Follow the usage guidance above for hair, scalp and skin.

Storage

Keep the cap tightly closed and store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and heat. Wipe the tip clean after each use.

Safety

Intended for external use only. Do a small patch test before first use and avoid contact with eyes. Stop using it if irritation occurs. If you are pregnant or nursing, or have a skin or scalp condition, check with your healthcare provider first.

Keep out of reach

Keep out of reach of children. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.
